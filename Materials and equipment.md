# Materials and equipment

Use this as a starting list. Change quantities to fit your class size.

## Printer
- Brand / model: to be updated at later date
- Enclosed printer recommended
- 0.4 mm nozzle
- Spare PEI or textured build plate
- Spare 0.4 mm nozzles

## Filament
- PLA: eSun (recommended) — https://esun3dstore.com/
- PETG: PETGUSA (recommended) — https://petgusa.com/
- Diameter: 1.75 mm
- Start with PLA. Use PETG only when a teacher chooses it.
- Dry box or airtight bins with desiccant recommended, especially for PETG

## Computers and software
- Student computers or shared laptops with a browser
- OrcaSlicer
- Tinkercad (browser)
- Access to MakerWorld and Printables
- USB drives or a shared folder for student files

## Hand tools
- Flush cutters
- Plastic scrapers or plastic pry tools
- Needle-nose pliers
- Calipers (digital, mm)
- Rulers
- Small files or sandpaper for cleanup
- Gloves for handling sharp support material

## Classroom extras
- Isopropyl alcohol and paper towels for plates (if your printer manual allows it)
- Glue stick or plate adhesive only if your plate needs it
- Storage bins: prints, reprints, hardware
- Labels and a print-queue sheet
- Power strip and a stable table

## Safety
- First-aid kit
- Posted safety rules
- Hair ties
- Nozzle and bed are hot. Keep hands out of a moving machine.

## Optional
- Spare Bowden tube or extruder parts if your machine uses them
- Camera for documenting prints
- Small scale for leftover filament
