# Lesson 7 — Two-part objects and clearance

**Time:** 45 minutes main lesson  
**Goal:** Students design two parts that work together, leave a real gap, and slice both for the Q2.

## Learning targets
1. Open Lesson 7 from the student repo.
2. Split one useful object into two printable bodies.
3. Use 0.3–0.4 mm clearance so a lid, peg, or clip can fit.
4. Give each part a flat face and walls at least 2 mm thick.
5. Export two STLs or one 3MF with two objects.

## Design challenge — pick one
- Box and lid: lid opening is 0.4 mm larger than the box lip
- Hook and back plate: pegs or screw holes line up
- Stand base and phone stop: stop slides or rests on the base

Max assembled size: 80 x 80 x 50 mm. Both parts should print without a forest of supports.

## Teacher prep
- [ ] Demo two lids: 0.0 mm clearance (stuck) and 0.4 mm (works)
- [ ] Show Duplicate and Align in Tinkercad
- [ ] Lesson 6 measured prints on the table
- [ ] Lesson 7 student file is in the student repo

## Agenda

### 0:00–0:05 — GitHub + pickup
Open Lesson 7.

### 0:05–0:13 — Fit is a number
Hold up a stuck lid and a working lid. Write 0.4 mm on the board.

### 0:13–0:20 — Demo
Build a 40 x 30 x 15 mm box. Duplicate it. Turn the copy into a lid. Make the inner lid 0.4 mm larger. Keep both flat.

### 0:20–0:42 — Students build
Checkpoint: two separate bodies, clearance written down, each part has a flat face.

### 0:42–0:45 — Queue
YYYY-MM-DD_Firstname_Box_v1.stl and Lid, or one 3MF with both objects.

## Success check
