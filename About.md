# About this project

This is an open-source lesson plan for starting a 3D printing class.

Anyone can copy it, teach from it, and change it to fit their students. That is the point. It is meant to be shared and edited.

The class gives kids a real skill and a real machine to learn on. The hope is they find something they care about making, and put their energy there.

This is a work in progress and is meant to be used as an outline and place to get started. At the end of the day you will need to bring passion and creativity to the table. I just want to create a starting point for the next person who will take this on.

## How to use it
- Teachers use this repo for full lesson plans.
- Students use the student repo for worksheets.
- Change what you need. Keep credit if you remix the plans.

## Class tools
- Printer: QIDI Q2
- Slicer: OrcaSlicer
- CAD: Tinkercad
- Models: MakerWorld and Printables
- Filament: PLA or PETG unless you choose otherwise
