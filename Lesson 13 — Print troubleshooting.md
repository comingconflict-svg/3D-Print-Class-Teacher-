# Lesson 13 — Print troubleshooting

**Time:** 45 minutes main lesson  
**Goal:** Students name a print problem, pick one likely cause, and try one fix.

## Learning targets
1. Open Lesson 13 from the student repo.
2. Match a failed or weak print to a cause: first layer, orientation, supports, thin walls, scale, or filament.
3. Change only one thing.
4. Slice the repair on the Q2 profile.

## Common classroom problems
- Print peeled at the corner: dirty plate, no brim, tiny footprint
- Spaghetti: knocked over or first layer failed
- Weak clip: 1 mm wall
- Hole too tight: no clearance
- Ugly underside: supports on a face they care about

## Teacher prep
- [ ] Scrap box of failed prints
