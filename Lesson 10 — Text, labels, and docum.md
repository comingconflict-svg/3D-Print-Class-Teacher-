# Lesson 10 — Text, labels, and documenting a print

**Time:** 45 minutes main lesson  
**Goal:** Students add readable text to a part and write a short credit note.

## Learning targets
1. Open Lesson 10 from the student repo.
2. Add initials or a short label that will survive printing.
3. Keep raised text about 1 mm high and at least 1 mm line width.
4. Avoid tiny text on the first layer.
5. Group text into the body before export.

## Text rules
- Two or three characters beat a full sentence
- Inset text of 0.4 mm often fills in; 0.8–1.0 mm is safer
- Do not put tiny text on a curved face this lesson
- Group text into the body before export

## Teacher prep
- [ ] Good and bad text samples
- [ ] Lesson 9 strong prints on the table
- [ ] Lesson 10 student file is in the student repo

## Agenda

### 0:00–0:05 — GitHub
Open Lesson 10.

### 0:05–0:15 — Why text fails
Show filled-in letters and letters that fell off in Orca. Repeat: Group. Thickness. Face choice.

### 0:15–0:38 — Add a label
Students add initials to a current object or a 50 x 20 x 4 mm tag if last lesson’s file is missing.

### 0:38–0:45 — Document
Sheet needs file name, remix credit if needed, and one next change.

## Success check
Grouped text. Preview does not show floating letters.
